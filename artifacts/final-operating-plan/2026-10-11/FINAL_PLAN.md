## One offer

A £745 one-off research pack for a supplier making an adult social care territory decision in England. Start with 25 distinct CQC provider IDs within the published 25–50 range, only after eligibility is proven. Confirm tax treatment before quoting. Use one agreed region or local authority and explicit service taxonomy, selection predicates, exclusions and any commercial-group roll-up.

Deliver an executive PDF (target three to five pages, with a labelled appendix if needed), CRM-ready CSV and source/licence manifest. Each entry has source-linked facts, provider and relevant location IDs, a fit explanation, a qualification question and uncertainty. No promised contact enrichment, budget, staffing shortage, future inspection, compliance opinion, exclusive intelligence or guaranteed sale.

Preferred first cohort: the verified relevant compliance/quality consultancies in the tracker, then training suppliers in the same region. This changes the earlier training-first hypothesis; compare actual buyer response rather than assuming one segment is better. West Midlands is a working example until selected.

Terms used here: a provider is a registered organisation; a location is a registered care site/service. A gate is a check required before proceeding. A gate receipt is the evidence supporting its approval. A file hash identifies the exact version that was reviewed.

## No-supported-scope path

If fewer than the agreed minimum qualify, stop before payment. Offer a supported wider scope or refuse. A smaller pack or different price is a separately approved commercial exception with revised terms and buyer acceptance; it is not an automatic fallback. Missing evidence stays missing. Never pad a shortlist or relabel locations as organisations.


## Payment exception path

For mismatch, failed, duplicate or uncertain payment, open a case with a merchant owner and update date. Do not ask the buyer to pay again until the first attempt is resolved. Any refund or live merchant change needs the separate existing authorisation. Scope changes require a new version and acceptance; they cannot silently change what the paid buyer receives.

## Reviewer checklist for each pack

Reconcile eligible set and shortlist count to the accepted predicates. Check provider/location grouping, every source URL, factual date, current-versus-change meaning, evidence edition and uncertainties. A location rating must not be stated as an organisation-wide rating. Check the fit explanation and qualification question without inventing procurement need.

Open the actual PDF and CSV; reconcile counts/fields between them; check readability, import-safe CSV, empty values and unsupported filler. Verify attribution in the PDF and manifest, with CQC and the applicable Open Government Licence link. Confirm the recipient inventory contains the reviewed manifest as well as PDF and CSV.


## After receipt

Record product acceptance separately: the buyer confirms the pack meets the accepted scope, or identifies a defect. Follow up on usefulness five working days after confirmed access. Ask which decision or action the pack supported and what remained missing. A compliment, download or silent buyer does not establish product acceptance or impact.

Recovery remains owned until resolved. An access issue gets acknowledgement by next working day and a realistic next-action date. A factual defect freezes affected release/new commitments, receives an independent correction review, and follows the operative remedy. Do not invent a refund entitlement or guaranteed fix deadline in a template.

# Source and product work in the right order


## Current-status scope is a proposal to prove

Local code separates Brief checkout flags from the Radar readiness helper. But the inspected Brief generator only includes rows with supported events in its time window. Removing change-based language does not turn it into a complete current-status selector. A current-status manual method needs approved selection/ranking, complete fields, lineage and full output review; it cannot be a gate bypass.

Registration dates do not prove physical openings. “Not rated” has several possible meanings. Current ratings and dates must retain their published location/service level. Source edition age, effective fact date and time of observation are different. Weekly directory fields alone may not satisfy every requested fact; prove actual inputs before promising them. [S3]
