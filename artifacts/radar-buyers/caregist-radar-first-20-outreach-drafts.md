## Candidate 1: CQC Consultants

Status: candidate only; current fit and permission not verified.

Subject: CareGist research for your England territory

<!-- EMAIL_BODY_START -->
Dear CQC Consultants team,

CareGist offers two services based on public CQC information.

The Territory Opportunity Brief costs £745 once and provides research for an England territory agreed in writing: up to 50 distinct provider organisations to investigate, with the exact number confirmed before payment, factual selection reasons, a CSV dataset, a three to five page summary, and source links and observation dates.

The Weekly Digest pilot costs £150 in total for four weekly issues covering one agreed England region, delivered by email and PDF. It reports new registrations and newly published CQC ratings with official links, source edition dates and observation windows. It ends after four issues with no automatic renewal. Quiet weeks may have no matching items.

Would either format be useful for a territory or research question your team is considering? If so, please tell us the England area and question you have in mind. We would check source availability and agree scope and delivery arrangements in writing before sending a payment request.
