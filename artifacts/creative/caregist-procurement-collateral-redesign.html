Every month, new care providers in England move through registration and must choose a care management platform before their first inspection. They are actively in-market, unbranded, and unreachable through your normal channels — they don’t attend trade shows yet, they aren’t on switch-vendor lists, and by the time they appear in public data they have already bought. CareGist works with these providers from pre-registration onwards through a structured Launch Procurement Audit, which means we know what they need, their budget, and their decision deadline before you ever pick up the phone.

Why the price is fair — the market context: Generic UK pay-per-appointment providers charge anywhere from £20 for unqualified bookings to £200+ for complex B2B, and fully qualified, decision-maker-verified B2B meetings trade internationally at £240–£600 each. Our pilot rate of £175 sits deliberately below the qualified-meeting market despite being higher-intent than anything a generic agency can produce, because we want conversion data as much as you do.

The supplied offer in one sentence

Launch Procurement Audit

A free 45-minute Launch Procurement Audit that maps every purchase a new provider must make on the road to registration and first placements, sequences it against their CQC timeline, and ends with a written procurement plan plus a curated shortlist for whichever decision is due next.

Supplied operating principle: The audit is genuinely free and genuinely useful even if the provider buys nothing through us. It is also our qualification engine: every supplier appointment CareGist books is backed by an audit, which is what lets us charge suppliers premium rates. Never compromise the audit’s honesty to force a booking — the audit relationship is the asset.
