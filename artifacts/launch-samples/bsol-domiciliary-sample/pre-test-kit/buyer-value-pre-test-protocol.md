# Buyer value pre-test protocol

## Purpose

Test whether the Birmingham and Solihull sample changes a real buyer's decision before broad outreach. Henry runs each test. No automated agent sends the sample, contacts a buyer, or records a payment ask.

The test covers 5–8 real buyers. It is complete only when each participant has reviewed the sample and answered the payment and decision questions in a live conversation or a direct written response.

## Who qualifies

A test buyer must meet every condition:

- Works for a recruitment, staffing, or other supplier business selling into domiciliary care.
- Is active in Birmingham or the West Midlands.
- Is a named decision-maker, such as a sales director, marketing director, business development lead, or principal.
- Makes or materially influences territory selection, account prioritisation, or sales-resource decisions.
- Agrees to review the sample and give direct feedback.

Do not count an anonymous inbox, a care provider buying for itself, a person outside the target territory, or someone with no role in the decision.

Before any email, Henry confirms the legal entity type and uses the approved compliance route. A public email address is not consent. Sole traders and ordinary partnerships are not emailed without consent or a valid soft opt-in. Calls require TPS and CTPS screening.

## Test sequence

1. Confirm the buyer fits the qualification rules.
2. Ask how the buyer currently decides which care organisations to approach.
3. Show or send the sample only after the buyer accepts it.
4. Book the feedback conversation when the sample is accepted.
5. In the feedback conversation, ask these exact questions:

   - "Would you pay £745 for this?"
   - "What decision would it change for you?"
   - "What is missing?"

6. Ask what the buyer used, which records caused an action, what action followed, and who else reviewed it.
7. Record every objection word for word. Do not summarise it into CareGist language or argue with it before it is logged.
8. Classify the result only after the buyer has answered.

## Scoring

- `would-pay`: The buyer accepts a concrete £745 price for the stated Territory Opportunity Brief and identifies a decision or use. Record whether the commitment is immediate or conditional.
- `maybe`: The buyer will not accept the concrete price yet, gives a condition, or offers only polite future interest. Record the missing evidence, feature, trust condition, timing, or budget issue verbatim.
- `no`: The buyer declines £745. Record the reason verbatim and the alternative they use today.

Do not turn "I'd buy this again" or "looks useful" into `would-pay`. A payment only counts when received. This pre-test measures willingness and decision value, not revenue.

## Record keeping

Use `pre-test-tracker-template.csv`. Give each contact an internal ID. Record company, segment, entity type, named decision-maker, sample and callback status, the exact objection, dates, payment response, and source evidence. Do not add unverified names or contact details to the template.

Keep the source wording intact. For example, record "our sales team already has this in Bullhorn" rather than "CRM integration objection". The verbatim statement is the evidence.

## Commercial rule

A scoping conversation comes before any payment ask. There is no Stripe URL in this kit. Live Stripe objects and Payment Links remain a founder gate. Henry sends, calls, and makes any later authorised payment ask.
