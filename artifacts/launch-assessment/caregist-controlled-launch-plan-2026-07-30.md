# CareGist controlled launch plan

**Decision:** Do not launch the four-page collateral or the five-demo pilot as written. Launch a narrow, evidence-led validation programme, then a one-vendor paid proof-of-concept if the evidence passes.

## Proposed launch wedge

Replace the broad “14 purchases / full procurement concierge” launch with one narrow initial service:

> **CareGist Software Readiness Review:** a structured review for recently registered care providers that identifies whether a live care-management software decision remains, documents requirements and timing, and—only when the provider asks—introduces suitable vendors.

Use “recently registered providers” unless CareGist later proves a lawful pre-registration source. Do not claim privileged CQC access, CQC endorsement, a fixed purchase count, a mandated first-inspection software deadline, or a vetted shortlist before the corresponding evidence/control exists.

## Step 1 — Human Gate 1: approve validation scope, not launch

Henry approves or rejects only this scope:

- internal preparation and primary-source verification;
- no publication of existing PDF;
- no five-demo promise;
- no approved price yet;
- no recording or supplier sharing;
- no spend beyond a separately approved cap;
- one software category only;
- one vendor maximum at proof-of-concept stage.

**Exit evidence:** effective Gate 1 record identifying entity, project, country, scope, budget cap and exclusions.

## Step 2 — Resolve entity and authority

Verify the one entity that will be controller, contracting party, invoice issuer and CareGist brand/IP user. Reconcile H-Kay and N Dumane Consultancy Ltd references. Evidence company authority and brand/IP rights.

**Required artifacts:** entity decision; Companies House evidence; brand/IP assignment or licence; approved company details block; bank/invoice ownership mapping.

**Stop condition:** any unresolved conflict between controller, contracting party, payment recipient or IP owner.

## Step 3 — Create the verified UK operating pack

Legal/Compliance produces a versioned UK obligations register covering CQC/OGL data, UK GDPR/DPA, PECR channels, recording, supplier sharing, direct marketing, CAP claims, VAT display, contract/complaints and retention. Qualified UK legal/accounting review is required for binding conclusions.

**Required artifacts:** verified UK Country Pack; source register; risk register; approval register; owner/due-date matrix.

## Step 4 — Define the exact CQC data boundary

Create a field inventory showing source URL/dataset, field, update cadence, permitted use, attribution, personal-data status, freshness and correction logic. Define “recently registered” precisely. Never transform a registration date into proof of current buying intent.

**Required artifacts:** CQC/OGL assessment; attribution standard; freshness label; correction workflow; prohibited-claim list.

## Step 5 — Design privacy before contact

Map every processing event: prospect research, outreach, structured review, notes, matching, provider instruction, vendor brief, follow-up, suppression and deletion. Choose and approve lawful bases; do not bundle recording, storage and sharing into one statement. Start without call recording unless a documented necessity and compliant workflow are approved. Use structured notes with provider confirmation.

**Required artifacts:** data map; privacy notice; LIA/DPIA decision; channel matrix; TPS/CTPS and suppression SOP; retention; provider-introduction instruction; vendor-recipient terms; rights/complaints workflow; no-personal-data-to-AI control.

**Stop condition:** missing privacy notice, suppression check, source verification, introduction instruction or retention rule.

## Step 6 — Narrow and operationalise the service

Replace the 45-minute “map every purchase” audit with a testable software-only review. Test 20- and 30-minute formats during provider discovery; do not promise a fixed duration until observed. Define a modest output: requirements summary, decision timeline and next-step options. Do not promise a comprehensive procurement plan or vetted shortlist.

**Required template fields:** provider/service identity; role/authority; current system/status; service type; required capabilities; integration constraints; user count/scale band; budget band (optional); decision deadline; decision team; provider-selected next action; permission status.

## Step 7 — Define billable-demo evidence before selling

A future demo is billable only if one immutable case pack proves: approved terms; completed review; all qualification criteria; provider-requested introduction; decision-maker role; vendor pricing version and budget-fit method; timing evidence; privacy/sharing status; brief delivery; neutral attendance; vendor acceptance or expired objection window; complaints; QA; invoice linkage.

Vendor objection must be time-limited and based only on objective missing/false evidence—not non-conversion. Define replacement cap, pilot end and independent dispute reviewer.

## Step 8 — Vendor discovery, not a sales promise

After outreach controls pass, interview 5–8 of the 11 researched CareTech vendors. Do not send the current PDF. Use a short discovery brief.

Ask: acquisition method; value of recent-registration timing; existing lead costs; exact qualification; required evidence; objection window; acceptable price range; minimum account value; sales cycle; conversion expectations; willingness to co-design.

**Pass thresholds:** three buyers accept substantially the same qualification/evidence standard; two show credible willingness to pay around the intended economics; one accepts a concrete conditional design-partner next step. Courtesy interest does not count.

**Fail/redesign:** no common evidence definition; timing is considered too late; no credible willingness to pay; vendors demand outcome-based billability.

## Step 9 — Provider problem discovery

After provider-outreach controls pass, conduct 8–12 permission-led interviews with recently registered providers. Do not share details with vendors and do not present a shortlist.

Measure: whether software is already selected; live decision within 90 days; useful review duration; desired written output; comfort with supplier funding; willingness to disclose requirements/budget; willingness to request an introduction; trust/privacy concerns.

**Pass thresholds:** six completed interviews; at least half retain an unresolved near-term software decision; at least four ask for a vendor introduction after disclosure; no serious privacy/trust complaint.

**Fail/redesign:** most already bought; insufficient unresolved need; providers perceive disguised lead generation; review labour is excessive.

## Step 10 — Measure labour and rebuild economics

Track minutes for research, outreach, review preparation, call, notes/output, matching, booking, brief, QA, attendance evidence, invoice and support. Do not count founder time as free.

At £175 per accepted demo and £20/hour illustrative direct labour: absolute break-even is 8.75 hours per accepted demo before overhead. For 40%, 50% and 60% gross-margin targets, maximum direct labour is 5.25, 4.38 and 3.50 hours respectively. If observed labour exceeds the approved threshold, change scope/process/price through Henry's pricing gate or stop.

## Step 11 — Synthetic end-to-end rehearsal

Using synthetic data, execute and evidence: accepted case; provider no-show; vendor objection; false criterion; privacy/recording complaint; replacement; invoice; credit note; opt-out/deletion. Independent QA verifies fail-closed behaviour and no out-of-scope data exposure.

**Pass:** every case has traceable evidence, clear owner, response time and correct financial/data outcome; no Critical/High finding.

## Step 12 — Prepare four separate controlled artifacts

1. Vendor discovery one-pager—hypothesis and evidence standard, no unsupported benchmark.
2. Provider invitation—narrow software review, supplier-funding disclosure, provider-controlled introductions.
3. Internal SOP—scripts, evidence, privacy, matching, complaints, QA and stop rules.
4. Draft vendor order/SOW—acceptance, objections, replacements, term, data, liability, price/tax and disputes.

Claims register links every objective claim to current evidence and owner. Independent QA reviews final copy, mobile/desktop/PDF, source fidelity and current hashes.

## Step 13 — Human pricing and contract gate

Finance supplies observed labour and downside/base/upside economics. Legal/accounting supplies entity/VAT/terms review. Henry alone approves or rejects price, commitment, replacement exposure and contract. Do not infer approval from the old £175 draft.

## Step 14 — First paid proof-of-concept

Recommended risk envelope for Henry's consideration: one vendor; up to three accepted demos; pay per accepted demo; no setup fee only if approved; no exclusivity; no conversion commission; no guaranteed minimum; fixed end date; objective objection window; replacement cap; explicit stop conditions. This is a recommendation, not approved pricing or terms.

## Step 15 — Weekly control loop

Track separately: prospects contacted; discovery completion; providers with unresolved need; introduction requests; qualification by criterion; booking/attendance/acceptance; objection reasons; replacements; labour by stage; evidence completeness; revenue earned; invoices issued; cash received; overdue/disputed amounts; provider/vendor satisfaction; complaints/privacy incidents; trial/contract outcomes.

**Immediate stop:** privacy/recording incident; missing introduction instruction; serious complaint; missing billable evidence; uncontrolled overdue invoice; vendor rejects based on sales outcome; labour breaches ceiling; recommendation distorted by vendor economics.

## Step 16 — Scale decision

After the proof-of-concept, Strategy, Finance, Compliance, Customer Success and Independent QA return evidence. Henry decides: stop; redesign; repeat one-vendor proof; or approve controlled scale. Scale only with positive observed contribution, provider trust, vendor acceptance, no Critical/High findings and complete legal/data/finance controls.

## Ownership

- Strategy: interviews, evidence synthesis, positioning and thresholds.
- Legal/Risk/Compliance: Country Pack, data/marketing/claims/contracts screen.
- Finance/Admin: labour economics, VAT/invoice readiness, AR and dispute controls.
- Customer Success: provider journey, support, complaints and retention.
- Engineering/Data Security: system of record, access, retention, suppression and evidence integrity.
- Creative: separate final assets only after claims are approved.
- Independent QA: separate-provider review of evidence, operations and collateral.
- Henry: Gate 1, entity/authority decisions, pricing, contracts, personal-data decisions, publishing/spend and final launch.

## Final operating principle

Do not sell “qualified demos” before CareGist has proved it can identify an unresolved need, earn provider permission, produce objective evidence and deliver the work inside a profitable labour ceiling. First prove the mechanism; then sell the outcome.
