# Manual Territory Brief: Sibongile's pilot runbook

11 October 2026. Proposed operating instructions for review. No role is staffed or external route approved by this document. Sibongile is the user-nominated operator with 25 hours/week. Henry is the proposed commercial/merchant owner. Independent reviewer and backup remain to be assigned.

**Operating boundary**

Manual means a person owns scope, research and delivery decisions. It does not mean payment, source, export, privacy or review gates can be bypassed. Use an approved rehearsal environment and synthetic buyer before any paid service. Drafts remain internal until the appropriate outbound/output approval. Do not collect card details or place credentials/customer data into a public repository.

Use one restricted order register and one restricted evidence folder per order in an approved existing tool. The template in this directory is blank and safe to keep in Git; real buyer records belong in the restricted operational store. Give each enquiry a unique ID; create a linked order only after scope acceptance. Keep the original incoming email, written scope version and each subsequent change.

**Daily rhythm: five hours, five working days**

| Time budget | Work |
|---|---|
| 30 minutes | Check enquiries, payment exceptions, deadlines, failed delivery and source status; acknowledge or escalate. |
| 60 minutes | Qualified buyer research/conversations/follow-up within authorised outreach scope. |
| 150 minutes | Produce/review one active Brief, or run the rehearsal while gates are closed. |
| 30 minutes | Reviewer handoff, delivery/access check and customer acceptance follow-up. |
| 30 minutes | End-of-day inbox check, order log, time record and next-day plan. |

Reallocate for scheduled buyer calls and correction work; preserve daily inbox/deadline coverage. The first week uses the separate setup allocation in PLAN.md. The independent reviewer has separately reserved time. If Sibongile is absent, the named backup takes the queue; otherwise Henry pauses new commitments and handles existing deadlines.

**Order progression and evidence**

| State | Entry evidence | Action | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENQUIRY_RECEIVED | Message actually in inbox; receipt time; enquirer | Assign owner, ID and acknowledgement due date | Acknowledgement recorded; scope conversation scheduled or scoped rejection |
| QUALIFYING | Buyer contact and stated decision | Establish service sold, region, provider/service categories, exclusions, budget authority and deadline | Written scope draft; relevance/feasibility questions resolved |
| FEASIBILITY_CHECKED | Source edition, source path and candidate set | Validate distinct organisations, inclusion rules, unsupported signals and capacity | Operator and independent source reviewer verdict; no unsupported promises |
| SCOPE_ACCEPTED | Buyer acceptance of versioned order/scope and actual date | Reserve slot; ensure money/source/review gates approved | Accepted version, fee/terms, recipient, start/date rules and approval IDs |
| PAYMENT_REQUESTED | Approved merchant route and order | Issue once through authorised operator; reconcile exceptions | Merchant reference tied to this order; no work marked paid without merchant evidence |
| PAID_VERIFIED | Merchant-confirmed amount, GBP, product/order and payer match | Confirm receipt; record work-start timestamp when production begins | Payment evidence, customer confirmation and start record |
| IN_PRODUCTION | Validated input and approved scope | Produce source-bound CSV/PDF; track time and questions | Draft artifacts with matching counts and source manifest |
| QA_REVIEW | Complete draft and original scope/evidence | Independent reviewer checks every shortlisted record and buyer promise | PASS or explicit defect list; corrected files must be re-reviewed |
| READY_TO_DELIVER | Reviewer PASS; final version/hash; valid paid entitlement | Verify recipient, file permissions and approved delivery route | Delivery request linked to exact files and recipient |
| SENT | Provider send result, message ID or equivalent route receipt | Check access, bounce, wrong recipient and expiry issues | Actual receipt/access evidence; unresolved cases stay open |
| RECEIVED / ACCEPTED | Buyer receipt or tested access; explicit acceptance separately | Ask usability and decision-value questions; record any defects | Acceptance or issue record, actual hours and cost |
| RECOVERY_OPEN | Failure, complaint, rejection or cancellation | Assign owner/deadline; determine correction/date/refund under operative terms | Corrected and reviewed delivery, written revised agreement, or reconciled refund outcome |
| CLOSED | Accepted completion or documented final remedy | Retain minimum necessary evidence; follow retention policy | Closure reason, unresolved limits if any, final reconciliation |

Use separate fields for payment, work, delivery and remedy status as well as the overall state. A partial refund, failed email or prepared file cannot be represented accurately by a single “complete” checkbox. A declined/unpaid enquiry closes with its own reason; it is not a completed sale.

**Qualification script (15–20 minutes)**

1. What do you sell, to which provider/service types, and who makes the buying decision at your customer?
2. What territory decision or account-prioritisation task are you trying to complete, and by when?
3. How do you do that research today? Which sources and people are involved? What is slow or unreliable?
4. Which facts would make an organisation worth investigating, and which would rule it out?
5. Show three evidence-backed sample entries. Which would you actually investigate, and why? What is missing?
6. Does a one-off scoped pack at £745 fit that job? Who can approve the purchase? Ask for a concrete next step; record objections verbatim.

Do not steer buyers to say the pack is useful. Existing procurement budget, an accepted scope or payment is stronger evidence than hypothetical enthusiasm. The £745 price is the current offer, not an assertion that it is optimal. Do not silently include contact enrichment, exclusive leads, prediction, full compliance advice or extra territories.

**Feasibility and record verification**

Freeze region, service taxonomy, signal/status definitions, time window and exclusions. Count candidate locations and distinct CQC provider organisations separately. CQC provider grouping does not establish ultimate ownership or corporate groups. Treat uncertain parent relationships as unknown. A 25–50-organisation shortlist may have more location rows in its evidence CSV; document the relationship.

For each candidate retain: provider/location IDs, geography, service type, active-status check, official source URL, source edition, retrieved/observed time, relevant effective/report date, stated fact, reason it matches the agreed criteria, qualification question and uncertainty. A rating decline requires a valid before and after at the same entity/service level; otherwise call it a current rating or newly observed publication. Absence/archiving alone cannot establish a physical closure. An old rating does not establish an imminent inspection.

Recheck material facts before finalising. Exclude unavailable/conflicting evidence or make an agreed limitation explicit; do not replace unknowns with confident prose. If there are fewer than the agreed distinct organisations, stop before charging and propose a clearly specified alternative. Never broaden from homecare to clinics merely to reach a row target.

**Production and review**

Suggested eight-hour total order budget, to measure rather than assume:

| Activity | Total labour hours |
|---|---:|
| Allocated acquisition, including unsuccessful prospects | 1.00 |
| Enquiry triage and scoping | 0.75 |
| Source research and qualification | 2.50 |
| Analysis, CSV and executive brief | 1.50 |
| Independent review | 1.00 |
| Payment/admin | 0.25 |
| Delivery and access follow-up | 0.50 |
| Corrections/support allowance | 0.50 |
| Total | 8.00 |

The reviewer receives the accepted scope, source records and exact output, not just a producer's summary. Review every shortlisted organisation: correct identity/entity level; criteria; dates; source support; question; uncertainty; grouping; relevant next action. Check the entire rendered PDF, all CSV columns and values, matching totals, link access and safe CSV import. A 3–5-page executive brief can have labelled evidence appendices; do not promise the whole file is five pages if it is not.

Check CQC/Open Government Licence v3.0 attribution, the licence link and source edition/date in the final PDF and in an approved accompanying CSV manifest/README. Preserve per-row source identifiers/links as agreed. Do not insert attribution prose as fake CSV data rows that break CRM import. Include the manifest/README in the delivery inventory and final hash record. This implements the source-metadata/attribution requirement in the current published terms; the reviewer must inspect the actual files.

One hour may prove insufficient for 50 entries. Record the actual review time and reduce throughput or change scope/price through an approved agreement. Acceptance criteria take precedence over the planning budget. The separate reviewer is not replaced by Sibongile asking her own producing model whether the output is correct.

**Payment and delivery**

Henry/authorised merchant operator chooses an approved payment route and gives Sibongile the least access needed to reconcile status. Do not issue an ad hoc link to circumvent closed commerce gates. Confirm amount, currency, order reference, payment status and payer before work. Record fee, actual processor cost and any refund separately from gross receipts.

The delivery operator sends only the reviewed version through the approved route to the agreed recipient. Retain file names/hashes, scope version, review receipt, message ID and access evidence. A prepared pack, provider acceptance, inbox receipt, download and buyer acceptance are different facts. Test that an unauthorised recipient cannot retrieve the pack. Do not promise five downloads/30 days or other access terms unless the selected approved route supports them and they are in the agreement.

**Failure and recovery**

| Failure | First action and owner | Closure evidence |
|---|---|---|
| No response to enquiry | Sibongile checks mailbox/assignment; Henry covers absence | Actual acknowledgement or documented contact failure; no inferred order |
| Insufficient or conflicting source before payment | Sibongile stops quote, reviewer checks source | Buyer-approved scope revision or explicit refusal; no payment taken |
| Late/unmatched/duplicate payment | Sibongile flags; merchant owner reconciles before starting | Merchant/order match and re-agreed date if required; duplicates remedied |
| Source fails after payment or output misses scope | Sibongile opens case, informs buyer through approved route and escalates to Henry | Reviewed correction, written revised agreement or refund under operative terms |
| Email bounce / link fails / wrong recipient | Stop further sends; verify buyer and entitlement, revoke misdirected access where supported; escalate | Correct access restored; incident/correction recorded; no public attachment workaround |
| Cancellation before work begins | Record request and actual work-start state; Henry decides under terms | Merchant refund submitted and final status reconciled; buyer notified |
| Refund pending/failed | Merchant owner tracks it; Sibongile keeps case open | Final merchant status and communication; do not claim bank receipt without evidence |
| Operator/reviewer unavailable | Henry activates backup or pauses intake and resolves existing dates | New owner or buyer-agreed remedy; no unattended paid queue |

Proposed internal target: acknowledge an operational problem by the next working day and assign a resolution owner/date. It is not an already-approved customer SLA. Don't promise that every correction fits the allowance or set limits that contradict operative remedies.

**Rehearsal checklist before paid activation**

Use the same proposed operational tools and roles, with synthetic customer/order data. Internal tabletop work can prepare the record now. Actual controlled-inbox sends and merchant test transactions need their explicitly authorised destinations and route. Retain evidence of inbox receipt, recorded enquiry, scope acceptance, merchant test payment, generation, independent review, actual external storage retrieval, correct-recipient receipt and acceptance. Exercise a rejected scope, duplicate payment evidence, bounce/link failure, source failure, cancellation/refund and replay after refund. Record what is simulated versus exercised. A tabletop email or mocked merchant result cannot close the external-service gate.

The existing prescribed Grok/DeepSeek and founder gates remain separate. The current review does not activate anything. Once approved, begin with one paid order, examine its complete evidence trail, then accept the next within capacity.
