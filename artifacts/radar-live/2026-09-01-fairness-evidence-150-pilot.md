## 3. Unit economics (effort per digest, per region)

**Estimate basis:** the manual fulfilment steps defined in `VA_MONDAY_REVENUE_PACK.md` §10 (pull the region's CQC register snapshot; record snapshot date/time and source URL; select new registrations/applicants and rating changes from public data only; draft digest with item, change type, provider, service type, location, public link, plain-English note; cross-check every item against its source link; format as PDF; send by email; log delivery). The verified Gloucestershire digest was compiled from the ~24MB official ODS ("Care directory with filters", 04 Aug 2026).

**Estimated effort (labelled estimate — not a measured cost):**
- Per digest (one region): **~1.5–2.5 hours** (download/parse ~24MB ODS, filter region, select and cross-check items, write notes, format, send).
- Pilot total (4 digests): **~6–10 hours**.
- Implied labour-equivalent at £150: **≈ £15–£25 per hour** — well below every evidenced care-sector consultancy rate in §2b (Cura £75/hr; R4C £50/hr floor; day rates £375–£1,800+).
- Per-region overhead (one-time): region configuration, ODS source verification, digest template — small fixed cost, amortised across the 4 weeks.
- Zero-change weeks cost the same effort (snapshot still pulled, verified, and the week reported honestly as no-change) — priced unit is the monitoring service, not the item count.

**Economics conclusion:** at £150 the pilot does not clear even the lowest evidenced consultancy hourly rate for the labour involved; it is a market-entry pilot price set to prove willingness to pay on a territory-monitoring unit, not a labour-margin price. That is fair to the buyer and does not misrepresent value.
