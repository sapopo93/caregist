## Choose which care accounts to review first

For a recruitment or staffing business deciding where to focus its homecare account research in an agreed England territory. Birmingham and Solihull is the existing sample territory.

The proposed order includes:

- A territory dataset in Excel, with an accompanying CSV for the agreed CRM import layout. It lists qualifying care locations, provider identifiers, service types, geography, recorded registration dates, available ratings, source links and the source edition/check dates. Missing fields remain marked as missing.
- A shortlist of 25 distinct provider organisations, with a stated selection reason for each. Each reason connects a checked public fact to your agreed account-selection criteria and states what still needs qualification. Locations and organisations are identified separately. This uses the lower end of the documented 25–50 range.
- A four-page executive brief explaining the territory's size and service mix, supported observations, the account-selection method and recommended next research steps. This order includes no movement or closure analysis.

The purpose is to give your team a reasoned starting list for account review. The brief does not establish that a care organisation has vacancies, needs agency staff, has a purchasing budget or intends to buy. It includes no guaranteed meetings, sales, continuous monitoring or automatic renewal.

**Documented price: £745 for one brief.** VAT treatment and the final amount payable must be confirmed before an order. No discount or alternative pilot price is offered here.

**Documented turnaround: 3 working days.** Agree the start and delivery date after scope and source checks, before accepting an order. A complete sample has now been built locally from September source editions. This demonstrates the bounded data route and output formats, but does not establish a turnaround guarantee for other territories or unagreed requirements.

**Next step:** review the clearly dated sample in a 15-minute scoping conversation, agree the territory, service types and account-selection criteria, then confirm the final scope and delivery date. Payment follows only after the delivery and commercial gates pass. A usage interview follows delivery within seven days.

The completed Birmingham/Solihull review sample contains 353 locations, 329 provider organisations and 25 shortlisted organisations. It uses CQC's 1 September monthly edition, with location/provider identity checks against the 2 September directory. One representative CQC page per shortlisted provider was checked on 8 September. It includes an Excel workbook, two CSVs and a four-page PDF. The sample's staffing-buyer criteria are illustrative and must be agreed or revised for your order. No buyer-specific CRM import has been tested.
