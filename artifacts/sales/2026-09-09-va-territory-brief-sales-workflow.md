# VA workflow: Territory Opportunity Brief

## The only product to sell today

**Territory Opportunity Brief, £745. Price excludes VAT.**

Public offer: `https://www.caregist.co.uk/territory-opportunity-brief`

Business terms: `https://www.caregist.co.uk/terms`

Do not offer the £495 Market Movement Report, £150 Radar pilot, Radar subscriptions, Feed or Enterprise products.

## Opening line for the VA

“CQC data is public. The Territory Opportunity Brief saves your team from filtering it, deciding which organisations fit your criteria, preparing a CRM file, tracing sources and writing the internal brief. You receive those five outputs for an agreed England territory for £745.”

## Who qualifies

Organisations selling into, advising or investing in UK care, including compliance advisers, recruitment and staffing firms, software, insurance and similar suppliers. The buyer must be purchasing for a business and have authority to accept the Business Terms.

## Before payment

1. Share the public offer page.
2. Ask for the England territory, the buyer's service or offer, the care segment, and the factual criteria their team wants used to select organisations.
3. Record the agreed territory and criteria in writing. This is the order scope.
4. Confirm internally that the public-source path is available for that scope. If it is not, do not issue payment or promise delivery.
5. Send the buyer the confirmed scope, the £745 price, the terms link and the delivery target: three working days after scope and source checks are complete.
6. Only then send the existing Stripe Payment Link.

## What the buyer receives

- A buyer-specific shortlist of 25 to 50 care organisations in the agreed England territory.
- A CRM-ready CSV or Excel dataset for the agreed shortlist, limited to fields available from the checked public sources.
- A three to five page executive brief covering the agreed criteria and factual reasons for inclusion.
- Source links and observation dates for the public CQC information used.

## Birmingham and Solihull example

Free public example: `https://www.caregist.co.uk/examples/birmingham-solihull/`

This is a free, dated format example. It is not a separate product and is not for sale. If you show it, say it demonstrates the structure and evidence trail only. A paid buyer receives a new brief for their agreed territory after the scope and source-path checks.

## Do not promise

- Buying intent, vacancies, budget, contract timing or a commercial result.
- A delivery date before the scope and source-path checks are complete.
- Freshness beyond the observation dates supplied with the completed pack.

## If the buyer cancels or the source check fails

Before work begins, cancellation receives a full refund. If a source issue after payment prevents the agreed scope, offer a revised delivery date or a full refund if an alternative scope is not agreed. The full terms are at the published Business Terms link.
