## Who this is for

UK compliance and quality-improvement consultancies that support CQC-regulated providers and need a clear weekly view of public CQC changes in one England region.

## What you receive

The proposed pilot scope is four weekly email/PDF digests for one agreed England region. The available 4 August Gloucestershire digest shows this format:

- new registrations recorded in the source edition; and
- ratings published in the source edition, with provider and location details.

Each digest states the CQC register edition and its seven-day window. The available fresh digest contains zero registrations and one rating published. A rating publication is not a rating change: the source edition carries only the latest rating and publication date. CareGist compiles the digest manually. It does not include software access, a dashboard, login, API, provider contact data, predictions, inspection forecasts, item-level public CQC links, or a guarantee of commercial results.
