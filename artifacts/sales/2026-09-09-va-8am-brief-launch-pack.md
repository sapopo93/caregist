# VA launch pack: Territory Opportunity Brief

## The one offer

Sell only the **CareGist Territory Opportunity Brief** at **£745**. Price excludes VAT.

The buyer is a UK care-sector supplier or compliance consultancy that needs to decide which care organisations in a defined territory deserve account research first.

After buyer scope is agreed and the delivery start is confirmed, the buyer receives:

1. A territory dataset in CSV and Excel format.
2. A shortlist of 25–50 distinct organisations, selected against the buyer's agreed criteria.
3. For each shortlisted organisation: a published fact, reason for inclusion, qualification question and stated uncertainty.
4. A three to five page executive brief explaining the territory, priorities, evidence and limits.

The documented turnaround is three working days. Confirm the actual delivery date only after scope and source checks.

Do not promise buyer revenue, vacancies, staffing need, budgets, buying intent, current compliance, director research, breach findings, live monitoring or software access.

## Qualification questions

Record the answers before a payment link is offered:

1. Which territory or local authorities matter?
2. Which care services and provider types matter?
3. Which buyer decision should the shortlist support?
4. What counts as a useful organisation to review first?
5. Who will use the workbook, and what fields do they need for their CRM?
6. Who owns the decision and approval to buy?
7. What delivery date would be useful?

If the buyer cannot answer questions 1–4, offer the sample view and arrange a 15-minute scoping conversation. Do not collect payment.

## Readiness check before any external send or payment request

The founder or delivery owner must mark every item true:

- Buyer criteria and territory are recorded.
- A current source check supports the requested scope.
- The delivery owner confirms a specific three-working-day delivery date.
- Customer terms and refund/cancellation wording are approved.
- The £745 Stripe payment link works and shows the correct product name and price.
- The payment page states that the price excludes VAT.

If any item is false, the VA can prepare records and book a scoping conversation but must not send a payment link or state that an order is accepted.
