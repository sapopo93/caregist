CARE QUALITY CONSULTANCY

We can help Commission new services in Adult Social Care whether it is Residential or Domiciliary Care. We will do this in line with
CQC, Commissioning, and Contracts expectations. We provide support with stream Care Provision, HR Documentation, and creating bespoke Policies and Procedures in place ready for you to operate.

Turn Around and Crisis Management of Care Services with Poor CQC Rating or in special measures.

A summary of our services is listed below:

  • Supporting CQC & Local Authority Regulatory or Contracting issues to maintain full Compliance
  • Operational Management and Support
  • Undertaking Quality Assurance and Care Audits with Detailed Action Plans to rectify shortfalls
  • Temporal/Relief Home Manager Position due to illness or Short Term Cover required for a Care Service
  • Conducting new CQC style Mock Inspections Using the 5 key questions and the Quality Statements
  • Assisting with the Commissioning of new Care Services i.e. Care Home or Domiciliary
  • Offer Mandatory training
  • Writing and Review of Policies and Procedures
  • Assistance with CQC Registrations and PIR (Provider Information Return) before CQC inspections
  • Review and Writing of Care Plans
  • Supply and Recruitment of Staff ie. Care Staff, Registered Nurses, Registered Managers
