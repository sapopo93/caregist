CQC Compliance

Achieving and maintaining GOOD CQC standards is essential for delivering high-quality care. At Vita Care, we provide expert support to help care providers navigate the complexities of CQC regulations, ensuring compliance and continuous improvement.

​

Our comprehensive services include Full Quality Audits to assess and enhance your care standards, Crisis Management to support you during challenging situations, and Quality Surveys to gather valuable feedback for improvement.

 

We also offer Auditing Support, helping you identify and address compliance gaps, and Embedding Attitudes to foster a culture of excellence within your team. Additionally, we guide organizations through the CQC Registration process, ensuring a smooth and successful application.

​

With our expertise, we help you not only meet but sustain high-quality care standards, giving you confidence in your compliance and service delivery.

Full Quality Audit

We complete at Full Quality Audit, covering the 5 Key Lines of Enquiry. We then supply you with a Report detailing our findings, including an Action Plan.

Crisis Management

We offer support during times of Crisis, whether it be Interim Management Support or Support to Address Failings identified by CQC.

Quality Surveys

We can perform Quality Assurance Surveys Resulting in a full detailed written report, inclusive of at a glance percent-age results charts.

Auditing Support

We can complete Individual Auditing of Certain Criteria or Provide the Documents for you to Complete yourself, such as:

  • Medication Audits

  • Care Planning

  • Environmental Checks

  • etc.

Embedding Attitudes

We are able to assist you with Embedding Good Attitudes towards the the following:

  • Openness & Transparency

  • Complaints & Concerns

  • Improvements Required

CQC Registrations

We are able to support with the following:

New Registrations

  • PIR Completion

  • Enforcement Action / Notice of Proposals which threaten the Service Registration
