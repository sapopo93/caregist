CQC Consultancy Service

A comprehensive service for care providers, by care providers.

At Delphi Care we believe in building lasting partnerships with our clients.

We don’t simply walk away when the job appears to be done, quite the opposite. Our team will help you set realistic targets and we all work together to achieve them.

Our CQC consultancy service is no different. We understand the pressures many care environments face when it comes to improving their CQC rating, and with our experts on hand, we’re confident that with our wealth of knowledge and experience, we can guide you through the inspection process.

There when you need us...

We have teams based across the UK allowing us to provide our expertise to care providers throughout the UK. We also forge strong bonds with our clients allowing our service to become a partnership.

Our CQC consultants can support you in making key decisions for your service while also providing training and action plans to ensure compliance with CQC standards. We work with you to improve your service’s rating.

Get in touch with us on 0330 133 3002 or email customer-care@delphi.care today to learn more about our services or our highly experienced task force. 

What does our CQC consultancy service include?

Mock CQC Inspections

Our mock CQC inspections are carried out by our team of experts who can provide you with a range of insights and guidance into improving your overall rating.

We will check the key values that real inspections will focus around, whilst providing you with support on areas your healthcare setting needs to improve on, and give feedback where you’re currently excelling.

Management Mentoring

We can provide coaching to various management roles within your care setting.

We believe if your management teams have the knowledge and tools to make better decisions, this in turn will have a positive impact on staff, clients, and residents.

Crisis Management

As former CQC inspectors and registered managers, our team can provide invaluable guidance on handling situations that appear to be out of our control.

We’ll ensure you have an effective plan in place that doesn’t breach regulations but will satisfy local authorities and ensure your care setting is in control should a crisis occur.

CQC Registration Support

If you’re a new provider, completing the CQC registration can be daunting.

However, as consultants who have a combined 200 years’ of experience we will walk with you every step of the way to ensure you meet the relevant requirements and continue to improve on your rating, even long into the future.

Speak with our team to learn more about CQC Registrations.
