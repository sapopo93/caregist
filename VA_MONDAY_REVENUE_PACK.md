## 1. What we are selling (inclusions)

A 4-week pilot of **CareGist Radar**: a weekly CQC intelligence digest, delivered by email as a PDF, covering **one region only**, aggregating public CQC register signals:

- New CQC registrations / new applicants appearing in the region (public register data only).
- Rating changes (upgrades and downgrades) for providers in the region.
- Provider name, service type, location and the public register reference where available.
- A short plain-English note per item (what changed, when, and the public source link).
- Delivered once per week for 4 consecutive weeks (weeks 1–4 of the pilot), by email with a PDF attachment.
- Manual fulfilment: the digest is compiled and delivered manually by the CareGist team. No customer login, dashboard, or software access is included.

**Buyer profile:** UK CQC / care compliance consultancies (the verified first-20 cohort in `VA_REVENUE_TRACKER.csv`). The buyer is a business (corporate recipient), not a consumer.

## 2. Exclusions (what we are NOT selling)

- No predictions of inspection timing, inspection outcomes, or future ratings. CQC inspection cycles are not predictable from public signals and we do not claim otherwise.
- No private, unpublished, or non-public CQC information. Only public register data.
- No lead lists of providers for the buyer to sell to, no contact details of providers, no personal data of any individual.
- No software platform, dashboard, login, API, integrations, or automation.
- No guarantee of revenue, referrals, or engagements from the digest.
- No guarantee of completeness: the digest reflects publicly visible signals at compile time; it does not claim to capture every change (see Limitations).
- No outbound work done on the buyer's behalf.
