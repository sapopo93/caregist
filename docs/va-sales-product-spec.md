## 1. What the VA must understand first

CareGist helps UK care-sector businesses find and act on new care provider opportunities.

The simple message is:

CareGist tells sales teams which care providers are newly registered, where they are, what type of service they run, and how to contact or prioritise them.

The VA should not talk like a software engineer. Do not explain how the system works behind the scenes unless the customer specifically asks and the enquiry is handed to a senior team member.

The VA should speak in business outcomes:

- Find new care provider leads earlier.
- Stop wasting time on stale lists.
- Focus on the right regions and service types.
- Give sales teams cleaner data for calls, emails, and follow-up.
- Help managers see new opportunities before competitors do.

Live website URLs the VA can give:

- Main website: https://www.caregist.co.uk
- Pricing: https://www.caregist.co.uk/pricing
- Search / New Provider Lead Feed: https://www.caregist.co.uk/search
- Get a lead list: https://www.caregist.co.uk/lead-list
- API information: https://www.caregist.co.uk/api
- Why CareGist: https://www.caregist.co.uk/why-caregist

## 2. Product order: what we sell first

There are two different buyer tracks:

- Demand-side buyers: companies that sell to care providers. Sell them the New Provider Lead Feed first.
- Supply-side buyers: care providers themselves. Sell them profile claiming and paid provider visibility.

### Leading product: New Provider Lead Feed

This is the main product the VA should sell first to companies that sell to care providers. On the website this appears under "New-provider intelligence plans" and the Search page is titled "New Provider Lead Feed."

Plain English explanation:

New Provider Lead Feed gives customers a list of newly registered care providers in England. The customer can filter by area, service type, and other practical sales criteria. They can use the list for outbound calls, email campaigns, territory planning, and sales follow-up.

What the customer gets:

- Newly registered care provider leads.
- Provider name, location, service type, phone, website where available, and key public details.
- Saved searches for the areas or care services they care about.
- Weekly updates or exports for their sales team.
- CSV or Excel exports depending on the plan.
- Team access on higher plans.

Why this is our leading product:

- It solves the clearest pain: sales teams need fresh leads.
- It is easy for a customer to understand in one phone call.
- It is cheaper and easier to buy than a custom report.
- It fits many customer types: suppliers, recruiters, training firms, consultants, brokers, and care-sector vendors.
- It can be sold as a monthly subscription.

Best opening line:

"CareGist helps your sales team find newly registered care providers before your competitors are already speaking to them."

Best buyer outcome:

"You get a cleaner, earlier list of care providers to contact, instead of relying on stale spreadsheets or manual searching."

Recommended plans to sell:

| Plan | Best for | Sales language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free, £0 | Evaluation only | "You can start free to search providers and test the data, but it is limited." |
| Alerts Pro, £49 + VAT/month | Monitoring existing providers and rating changes | "Best if you mainly want watchlists, rating-change alerts, and weekly monitoring." |
| Data Starter, £99 + VAT/month | One person or light weekly lead building | "A low-cost way to start receiving new provider leads and exporting lists." |
| Data Pro, £199 + VAT/month | Small sales team | "Best for a team that wants more filters, bigger exports, and shared working." |
| Data Business, £499 + VAT/month | Larger sales team or connected sales process | "Best when the customer wants higher volume, more team access, and deeper workflow support." |

Important plan rule:

- New-provider lead workflow starts at Data Starter.
- Alerts Pro is for monitoring and rating-change alerts, not heavy new-provider exports.
- Free is for testing only, not a recurring sales workflow.

### Second product: Provider Visibility Listing

This is worth selling now, but only to care providers, not to suppliers or recruiters.

Plain English explanation:

Provider Visibility Listing helps a care provider improve how their CareGist profile appears to families, commissioners, partners, and people searching for care services. The provider can claim their listing, check core details, and upgrade if they want richer information, photos, and stronger visibility.

What the customer gets:

- Free claimed listing with a verified badge.
- Ability to keep core profile details accurate.
- Public inspection response where available.
- Provider description on paid plans.
- Photos on paid plans.
- Virtual tour link on Provider Pro and Sponsored.
- Sponsored badge and stronger placement on Sponsored.

Why this is second:

- It improves the quality of CareGist's directory.
- It gives new providers a simple way to present themselves better.
- It creates a second revenue line from care providers, not only from companies selling to them.
- It is easier to introduce after saying: "You already have a CareGist profile; would you like to claim and improve it?"

Why it is not the leading product:

- Many new providers are focused first on registration, staffing, compliance, and occupancy.
- They may not pay unless they understand how CareGist helps them be found.
- It depends on trust: providers need to believe people will view their profile.
- It can be harder for a VA to sell cold unless the provider can see their existing profile page.
- The free claim should usually come before the paid upgrade.

Best opening line:

"You already appear on CareGist. I am calling to help you claim your profile and improve how your service appears to people searching for care."

Best buyer outcome:

"Your profile looks more complete and professional, with your own description, photos, and service information instead of only basic public details."

Recommended plans to sell:

| Plan | Best for | Sales language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claimed Listing, free | Any care provider | "Start by claiming your listing at no cost so your profile is verified." |
| Provider Pro Listing, £99 + VAT/location/month | Provider wants a stronger profile | "Best if you want to add a proper description, photos, and a virtual tour link." |
| Sponsored Listing, £149 + VAT/location/month | Provider wants more visibility | "Best if you want richer profile content plus sponsored visibility." |

### Third product: Filtered Lead List / Dataset Pack

These are visible on the live website and should be used when the buyer wants a one-off list or broader dataset rather than a monthly plan.

Plain English explanation:

A filtered lead list is a one-off request for a defined segment, such as a region, service type, or rating group. A dataset pack is for broader data buyers who want a larger file or regional pack.

What the customer gets:

- A way to request a filtered lead list from the website.
- A route to buy a full dataset or regional pack from the website.
- A lower-commitment option before a monthly subscription.

When to sell it:

- The buyer says they only want a list once.
- The buyer is not ready for a monthly subscription.
- The buyer asks for a regional file, one-off export, or sample data.

Best opening line:

"If you do not want a monthly subscription yet, you can request a filtered lead list or buy a dataset pack from the website."

Best buyer outcome:

"You can test CareGist with a defined list before committing to a recurring plan."

### Fourth product: API / Sales-System Workflow

This is visible on the live website, but it should usually be a handoff unless the buyer already understands integrations.

Plain English explanation:

API and sales-system workflows are for teams that want CareGist information to feed into their own sales tools or internal processes.

When to sell it:

- The buyer mentions CRM, RevOps, data team, automation, or internal systems.
- The buyer wants information delivered into their own workflow rather than manually downloaded.
- The buyer is considering Data Business or Enterprise.

VA-safe wording:

"If your team wants the data to connect into your sales system or internal workflow, that is usually a Data Business or Enterprise conversation. I can capture the requirement and arrange the right follow-up."

### Fifth product: Provider Intelligence Dossier

This is not clearly presented as a self-serve public website product today. The VA can mention it only as a possible follow-up or commercial-team discussion. The VA can also call it a "provider background profile" if the customer does not understand the word dossier.

Plain English explanation:

A Provider Intelligence Dossier gives more detail on one care provider, so the customer can decide whether that provider is worth serious sales attention.

What the customer gets:

- A richer profile of a provider.
- Company and ownership context where available.
- Publicly available business background.
- Signals that help the customer judge whether the provider is a good opportunity.
- Better preparation before calling a high-value prospect.

Why this is a follow-on product:

- Some customers only need fresh leads first.
- Dossiers are most valuable once the customer wants to prioritise the best leads.
- It helps customers avoid wasting time on weak opportunities.
- It supports higher-value sales conversations.

Best opening line:

"The lead feed tells you who is new. The dossier helps you decide who is worth calling first."

Best buyer outcome:

"Your team can focus on the providers most likely to be valuable, instead of calling every name on a list."

Recommended pricing language:

- "Deeper provider background information depends on availability and package."
- "I will capture that request and ask the commercial team to confirm what is available."
- Do not quote dossier pricing on a call unless the product owner confirms the live buying process.

### Sixth product: Strategic Reports and Enterprise Data

This is not the main phone-sale product. It is a handoff product.

Plain English explanation:

Strategic Reports and Enterprise Data are for organisations that need a larger market view, custom data, regional reports, procurement support, or a special data package.

Who it is for:

- Investors.
- Local authorities.
- Integrated Care Boards.
- Large suppliers.
- Large care groups.
- Lenders.
- Insurers.
- Consultants needing a white-label report.

Why this is not first:

- It takes longer to sell.
- It may need procurement, legal, security, or custom scoping.
- The VA should qualify the need and pass it to a senior salesperson.

Handoff phrase:

"That sounds like a custom data or enterprise requirement. I will take the details and arrange for the CareGist commercial team to follow up with the right scope and pricing."

## 3. Identified customer groups

### Customer group 1: Care equipment and supplier companies

Examples:

- Medical equipment suppliers.
- Nurse-call system suppliers.
- PPE suppliers.
- Care furniture suppliers.
- Laundry, hygiene, pharmacy, and facilities suppliers.

Why they care:

New care providers often need to buy equipment, supplies, systems, and service contracts early. If the supplier calls late, the provider may already have chosen another company.

Main product to sell:

New Provider Lead Feed.

Upgrade to sell:

Provider Intelligence Dossier for high-value prospects.

Simple pitch:

"You sell to care providers. CareGist helps you see which providers are newly registered, so your team can call earlier and build pipeline before competitors do."

Good discovery questions:

- "Do you currently buy care provider lists, or does your team search manually?"
- "How quickly do you know when a new provider opens in your target area?"
- "Which regions or service types are most valuable for your sales team?"
- "How much is one new customer worth to your business?"

Best close:

"If one new provider becomes a customer, the monthly cost can usually pay for itself. Would you like to start with Data Starter or Data Pro?"

Examples:

- Care recruitment agencies.
- Registered manager recruiters.
- Nurse recruitment agencies.

## 5. Call structure for the VA

### Step 1: Open politely

"Good morning, may I speak with [Name]? My name is [VA Name] calling from CareGist. We help companies selling into the UK care sector find newly registered care providers and build cleaner sales lists. Is now a bad time for a quick question?"

If calling a care provider:

"Good morning, may I speak with the person responsible for your public care profile or marketing? My name is [VA Name] calling from CareGist. You already have a CareGist profile, and I am calling to help you claim it and improve the information shown. Is now a suitable time?"

### Step 2: Ask discovery questions before pitching

Ask two or three only:

- "Do you sell to care homes, home care agencies, supported living providers, or all care providers?"
- "How do you currently find new care provider leads?"
- "Do you work nationally or by region?"
- "Is fresh lead generation a priority for your team this quarter?"
- "How many people would need access to the data?"

If calling a care provider, ask:

- "Are you the person who manages the provider's online profile or marketing?"
- "Is this for one location or more than one location?"
- "Would you like to add a better service description, photos, or a virtual tour link?"
- "Is your main goal profile accuracy, trust, local visibility, or more enquiries?"

### Step 3: Match the product

If they need new leads:

"The best fit is the New Provider Lead Feed. It gives your team newly registered care providers that you can filter and export for sales follow-up."

If they only want to test:

"You can start with the Free plan to search providers and test the data. If you want a proper recurring lead workflow, Data Starter is the first paid plan for that."

If they mainly monitor existing providers:

"Alerts Pro is the better fit if you mainly want watchlists, rating-change alerts, and weekly monitoring rather than new-provider lead exports."

If they need prioritisation:

"The next step may be deeper provider background information. I will capture that requirement and ask the commercial team to confirm what is available."

If they are a care provider:

"The best first step is to claim your listing for free. If you want to add richer information, photos, and stronger visibility, Provider Pro or Sponsored Listing would be the paid upgrade."

If they want a one-off list:

"If you do not want a monthly plan yet, the website has a Get a lead list option where you can request a filtered list."

If they want a dataset:

"If you want a wider file rather than a monthly workflow, the website has a Buy dataset option for full dataset or regional pack enquiries."

If they need custom reports:

"That sounds like an enterprise or custom report requirement. I will take the details and arrange a senior follow-up."

### Step 4: Explain price simply

"Data Starter is £99 plus VAT per month. Data Pro is £199 plus VAT per month and is usually better for a small team. Data Business is £499 plus VAT per month for larger workflows."

Full public pricing ladder:

"Free is £0. Alerts Pro is £49 plus VAT per month. Data Starter is £99 plus VAT per month. Data Pro is £199 plus VAT per month. Data Business is £499 plus VAT per month. Enterprise is contact sales."

For provider visibility:

"Claiming your listing is free. Provider Pro Listing is £99 plus VAT per location per month. Sponsored Listing is £149 plus VAT per location per month."

Do not over-explain. Quote only the plan that fits.

### Step 5: Close for next action

Choose one:

- "Would you like to start with Data Starter and test the lead feed?"
- "Would Data Pro be more suitable if more than one person needs access?"
- "Would you like me to book a short demo so you can see the type of provider leads included?"
- "Would you like to claim your provider listing first, then decide whether the paid profile upgrade is useful?"
- "Who else would need to approve this?"

## 7. Objection handling

### "We already have a list."

Response:

"That makes sense. Many teams already have some kind of list. The question is whether it is fresh enough. CareGist is useful when your team wants to see newly registered providers and act earlier, rather than working from older data."

### "We can search CQC ourselves."

Response:

"Yes, you can search public sources manually. CareGist is for teams that want to save time, filter opportunities, export cleaner lists, and make the process repeatable for sales."

### "Where does your data come from?"

Response:

"CareGist uses public Care Quality Commission provider information and turns it into cleaner search, monitoring, export, and workflow tools. CareGist is independent and is not CQC."

If they ask about refresh:

"The website describes CareGist as daily-refreshed care-provider data. We do not describe it as live occupancy, live pricing, or official real-time CQC source data."

### "Is this GDPR compliant?"

Response:

"CareGist is using public provider information for business-to-business care-market workflows. We do not sell private consumer care data. If you need a formal data protection answer, I can pass that to the CareGist team."

### "Do you cover Scotland, Wales, or Northern Ireland?"

Response:

"The core public positioning is based on CQC-registered providers. CQC covers England. Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland have separate care regulators, so I would need to pass any non-England requirement to the CareGist team."

### "It is too expensive."

Response:

"I understand. The usual way to judge it is against the value of one new customer. If one provider becomes a customer for your business, would that cover the cost of the plan?"

For care providers:

"That is fair. You do not need to start with a paid plan. The first step is to claim your listing for free. Then you can decide whether adding photos and richer profile information is worth upgrading."

### "Send me information."

Response:

"Of course. Before I send it, can I check whether you are more interested in new lead lists, provider background information, or a custom market report? That way I send the right details."

### "We are not interested."

Response:

"No problem. Just so I close the record correctly, is that because you do not sell to care providers, you already have enough leads, or the timing is not right?"

### "Are you part of CQC?"

Response:

"No. CareGist is independent. We are not CQC and we are not endorsed by CQC. We help businesses use care-sector provider information in a cleaner, more practical way."

### "Will this guarantee enquiries?"

Response:

"No, we cannot guarantee enquiries. The value is that your profile can look more complete, accurate, and professional when people view it."

## 8. What the VA must not say

The VA must not say:

- "We are CQC."
- "We are approved by CQC."
- "We guarantee leads."
- "We guarantee the customer will win business."
- "The data is perfect."
- "We know live occupancy."
- "We know live care-home prices."
- "We can provide any custom report immediately."
- "A paid profile guarantees enquiries."
- "Sponsored means CQC approval."
- "Sponsored means guaranteed top ranking everywhere."
- "VAT is included" unless a VAT-inclusive total is being clearly explained.
- "Dossiers include LinkedIn, Land Registry, job-board data, or private contact data" unless the current product owner has confirmed that specific feature is live and approved for sale.

Safe wording:

- "CareGist is independent."
- "CareGist helps with care-sector lead intelligence."
- "CareGist uses cleaned provider information."
- "CareGist uses public CQC provider data and packages it into search, monitoring, exports, and workflows."
- "CareGist is daily-refreshed, but it is not live occupancy or live pricing data."
- "Availability of deeper intelligence depends on the package and data available."
- "Provider visibility can improve how the profile appears, but it does not guarantee enquiries."
- "Custom requests are handled by the commercial team."

## 10. How to prioritise callbacks

High priority:

- They sell directly to care providers.
- They are a care provider and want to claim or improve their profile.
- They have a sales team.
- They currently buy lists or search manually.
- They work by territory.
- One new customer is worth more than £1,000 to them.
- They ask about exports, team access, or sales system use.
- They have photos, service information, or a virtual tour ready for their profile.

Medium priority:

- They are a small consultant or sole trader.
- They are a provider but only want to claim for free.
- They are interested but budget-sensitive.
- They only need one region.
- They ask for information before deciding.

Low priority:

- They do not sell to care providers.
- They only serve consumers.
- They have no budget and no clear sales use case.
- They are a provider but cannot confirm authority to manage the profile.
- They ask for custom public-sector procurement on a first call without a defined project.

## 12. Correctness rules for sales calls

The call is good only if:

- The VA explains the product simply.
- The VA asks questions before recommending a plan.
- The plan matches the customer need.
- Pricing is accurate and stated as excluding VAT.
- CareGist is described as independent from CQC.
- No future feature is promised as live.
- Provider visibility is not sold as a guaranteed enquiry product.
- The next step is clear.
- Sales notes are complete.

## 13. The main training message

Teach the VA this:

Do not sell "data".

Sell the result:

"Your sales team finds new care provider opportunities earlier, works from cleaner lists, and spends more time calling the right prospects."
