## Observed practice

The trainer demonstrates each task, then the learner repeats it independently. Mark each pass, fail or not observed with an evidence reference.

| Task | Exercise and acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer and scope | Explain that the buyer is a supplier researching care organisations. Agree area, inclusion rules, distinct provider IDs, files and acceptance before taking payment. |
| Coverage and rights | Inspect a synthetic source set with stale facts and duplicate locations. Reconcile provider count and rights; identify a shortfall and stop or revise scope. |
| Commercial authority | Find the current approved price/tax/terms decision. Distinguish a draft quote, enquiry, test transaction and actual authorised payment. Keep closed gates closed. |
| Build and independently check | Produce the agreed PDF, CSV and manifest. A different checker opens each, verifies claims/counts and records corrections. The maker cannot sign their own check. |
| Weekly Digest | Rehearse the £150 four-week manual pilot separately from the Territory Brief: scope, source-change checks, rights, four issue dates, quiet/incomplete weeks, independent checking and actual receipt. Explain no automatic renewal. |
| Delivery and receipt | Rehearse a private synthetic handover without sending messages or charges. Record the exact files, allowed destination, actual recipient opening and acceptance separately. |
| Customer explanation | Explain freshness limitations and that ratings/registrations do not prove buying intent. Do not promise sales, personal contacts, CRM imports or live subscriptions without their evidence. |

## Knowledge check

Ask the learner to explain the difference between source implementation, a local synthetic pass, a deployed release and an accepted operational/customer outcome. Ask how they handle wrong-organisation records, incomplete evidence, an uncertain save/delivery and an unavailable service. Require the approved escalation and secure contingency procedure, not a workaround using another person's account or fabricated completion.

## Sign off and retrain

Keep learner/name/role, trainer, date, exact target/revision, each task result, evidence reference, knowledge-check answers, failed/unobserved tasks, corrective coaching, reassessment date, learner acknowledgement and manager authorisation in the organisation's private training register. Do not put completed personal registers or credentials in this repository.

Pass only when every task relevant to the role and every knowledge question is demonstrated correctly. Failed or unobserved tasks require coaching and reassessment before independent operation. The supplied blank record is not proof anyone has been trained. Review competence after material workflow changes, incidents and identified gaps under the organisation's policy.
