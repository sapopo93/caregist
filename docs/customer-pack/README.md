# CareGist agreement and acceptance documents

Updated 15 October 2026. Use the [current operations manual](../manuals/OPERATIONS_MANUAL.md) to prepare a versioned scope and the [user manual](../manuals/USER_MANUAL.md) for the buyer journey. The previous link pointed to another machine-local checkout and was not a portable customer agreement.

An actual accepted order must identify the buyer and decision, geography, provider/service rules, exclusions and window, distinct-provider quantity, counting/ranking method, source/check date, required files, recipient, fee/tax basis, operative terms and agreed calendar delivery date. Record buyer acceptance of the exact version in the approved private system.

These manuals are operating instructions, not signed contracts. The existing [Brief terms draft](../territory-brief-terms-draft-FOR-SOLICITOR-2026-09-09.md) and [Digest terms draft](../weekly-digest-pilot-terms-draft-FOR-SOLICITOR-2026-10-01.md) remain drafts for qualified review. Do not treat them as legal approval or a completed customer agreement.

Keep pre-payment scope acceptance, merchant verification, independent review of exact final files, release authority, confirmed access and post-delivery acceptance separate. G0 to G5 must precede a paid commitment/payment request; G6 applies to the specific final release. Real customer details and signatures stay outside this public repository.
