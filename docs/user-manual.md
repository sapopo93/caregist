# CareGist user and fulfilment manual

> **Current authority:** See the [maintained 15 October revision](manuals/USER_MANUAL.md). The parallel draft below is retained for traceability; its pending steps, targets and claims do not supersede the final release record or current role manuals.

Version 1.0 · 15 October 2026 · Prospective buyers and authorised sales and fulfilment staff

CareGist helps suppliers research care organisations. Public directory information and scoped research outputs are evidence for investigation; they do not prove an organisation has budget, needs a service or will buy. The latest source and commercial gates remain the authority for availability. A deployed website does not open checkout, approve outreach or establish a completed paid order.

For the full current role-specific instructions, see the maintained [user, training, sales, CRM and operations manuals](manuals/README.md). This shorter guide covers the core order journey.

## Explore and enquire

Use the public site to inspect provider/location information, source dates and limitations. A provider can have several locations: count distinct provider IDs when agreeing organisation coverage. Verify important facts against the named sources before relying on them. Missing or stale information stays uncertain.

Submit an enquiry through the available product/contact route and retain its reference or confirmation. An enquiry is not an order, payment, entitlement or delivery receipt. If no confirmation arrives, use the published support route and check before submitting duplicates.

## Agree a research order

The current training workbook describes a Territory Opportunity Brief for an agreed area in England, with PDF, CSV and source/licence manifest. It records a proposed £745 price and 25 distinct provider IDs as the starting coverage aim within the stated 25–50 range. Those figures require the owner's current commercial/tax and feasibility confirmation before quotation. Historical £49 factsheet and Radar/Feed subscription materials describe superseded scopes and must not be mixed into this order.

Before payment, agree the buyer, area, inclusion/exclusion criteria, distinct-provider count, source dates, required files, licence/usage limits, turnaround, total price/tax treatment, acceptance and correction/refund terms. Confirm that enough qualifying providers exist. Do not silently substitute duplicate locations or unrelated providers for a shortfall. Change the agreed scope or stop with a clear explanation.

## Weekly Digest

The current catalogue also includes a Weekly Digest pilot at £150 for four weeks, delivered manually with no automatic renewal. Confirm the actual total and tax wording against the current approved catalogue/price manifest before quoting. Agree the territory, inclusion criteria, weekly coverage, source freshness and rights, delivery day, recipient and acceptance terms before taking payment. Follow the [pilot measurement protocol](weekly-digest-pilot-measurement.md) for baseline and each weekly run.

For each issue, verify the selected-source changes and scope, retain the source/rights references, independently check the actual report and record delivery and recipient acceptance separately. A quiet week or incomplete source collection must be labelled accurately; never invent a change to fill a digest. Track the four agreed issues and any correction/refund decision. A prepared email or scheduled run is not a delivered issue. Do not renew or add automated alerts without a separate agreed scope and approval.

## Staff fulfilment workflow

1. Qualify the enquiry and record the exact agreed scope privately. Preserve required owner approvals.
2. Check the selected authoritative sources, freshness, reconciliation and usage rights. Partial coverage is not a green gate. Do not activate a closed automated sale or collector merely to complete an order.
3. Accept payment only through the approved configured process after the named gates pass. Record actual payment separately from a checkout link, invoice or test transaction.
4. Produce the agreed PDF, CSV and source/licence manifest from the same scoped evidence. Deduplicate by the agreed provider identity and reconcile the final count, filters and exclusions.
5. Have a different authorised checker open all files, verify consequential claims against sources, check CSV usability and PDF readability and resolve defects. A producing model or pack author cannot approve its own output.
6. Deliver through the agreed authorised channel only when delivery is approved. Record the actual files/versions and receipt. Verify the buyer can open every agreed file and record acceptance, rejection and any correction separately.

The CSV is a spreadsheet-readable list, not a promised custom Excel workbook or CRM integration. Test an actual import before promising compatibility. Personal contact availability, live alerts, subscription entitlements, outreach permissions and sales results require their own accepted scope and evidence.

## Receiving and using a pack

Open every file and compare the area, qualifying criteria, unique-provider count, source dates and exclusions with your order. Check source/licence conditions before reuse. Ask for correction if files are missing, unreadable, duplicated or outside scope. Retain the agreed version and report exact rows/pages needing correction. This pack does not remove your responsibility to verify needs and comply with the rules for your intended outreach.

## Failures and support

An unavailable source, incomplete coverage, failed payment, missing entitlement, generation error or missing delivery receipt remains unresolved until checked. Do not relabel it successful or promise automatic retries/refunds without the accepted process. Keep customer details, payment credentials and personal contact data out of Git and training examples.
