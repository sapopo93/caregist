# CareGist user manual

Updated 15 October 2026. This guide explains the public directory and the two enquiry-led products. CareGist uses public CQC information about care services in England. It is independent of CQC. Check the official record before acting on care, compliance or commercial information.

## Search the directory

1. Open [Search](https://www.caregist.co.uk/search). Enter a provider name or place and use the displayed region, service and rating filters. Choose **Update search**.
2. Read the results and move between pages. If there are no matches, remove a filter or choose **Clear filters and start again**. An empty result is not proof that no service exists.
3. Open a result to inspect its profile, source dates and official CQC link. A registered provider is an organisation; a location is one registered site or service. Several result rows can belong to one provider.
4. Visit [Data status](https://www.caregist.co.uk/data-status) before relying on freshness or coverage. A working search does not establish that the latest directory edition has been fully reconciled.

A registration date does not prove a new business opened that day. A location rating applies to the stated location and inspection record; do not apply it to every branch. Factual segments and rankings do not establish buying intent, training need, available budget or a likely inspection.

If search is temporarily unavailable, retry later. If a record looks wrong, retain its URL, the disputed field and an official source link, then contact [support](mailto:support@caregist.co.uk). Do not include sensitive information about people receiving care.

Historical subscriptions, paid provider promotion and API plans are not current new-sale offers. Existing account entitlements are handled separately by support.

Include what your business sells, which decision the research should support, the region or authorities, provider/service types, exclusions and desired date. Do not send payment or card details with an enquiry. Coverage counts are a suitability check, not a reservation or accepted order.

## Agree the work before payment

CareGist checks source availability, supported selection rules, distinct-provider quantity and staff/reviewer capacity. You and CareGist must accept the same written scope, price, terms, recipient and calendar delivery date before a payment request through an approved route.

The Brief delivery target is three English working days after both written scope confirmation and source-path checking are complete. Count from the later check, with the following working day as day one; weekends and English public holidays are excluded. This is not a promise of three days from the first enquiry. Payment must be verified before production and does not restart the clock. If the date becomes unachievable, an explicit revised date must be agreed.

Henry approved human-led Weekly Digest sales at £150 once on 28 October 2026 and confirmed the existing Stripe setup. See the [founder decision](../decisions/2026-10-28-weekly-digest-sales-approval.md). The website enquiry alone does not place an order or take payment. The Weekly Digest needs its own agreed start date, region, four issue dates, format, terms and payment arrangements. Do not apply the Brief delivery clock to it. An enquiry or sample does not establish that either paid service is ready for your scope.

## Open and use a Brief

After authorised delivery, check that you can open all three items: PDF, CSV and source/licence manifest. Tell CareGist if any file is missing, denied or unreadable. Keep private access links private. A sent email or uploaded file is not confirmation that you received a usable pack.

Read the PDF's scope and limitations first. In the CSV, retain provider and location identifiers as text when importing into a spreadsheet or CRM; use UTF-8, check column mapping and inspect a few rows before importing the whole file. The CSV is not an Excel workbook, and compatibility with a particular CRM must be checked. Multiple locations do not multiply the distinct-provider total.

Follow each official source link and read the dates. Use the reason for inclusion to decide what to investigate. Treat the qualification question as an unanswered question, not a claim that the provider wants to buy. The manifest identifies editions, attribution and reuse limitations.

Confirm access to all three files, then separately confirm whether the pack meets the agreed scope. CareGist may later ask what decision it supported; neither receipt nor acceptance guarantees a sales outcome.

## Resolve access and account problems

Report the order reference, affected filename, error and time to support. Do not forward credentials, complete card data or private links into public channels. For an uncertain or duplicate payment, request reconciliation before paying again. Corrections, cancellations and refunds follow the actual agreed terms and authorised review.

Where an existing account is enabled, use the normal login and password-reset routes. If a session expires, sign in again; do not interpret cached plan labels or a checkout return page as payment or entitlement confirmation. Staff CRM access is a separate restricted workflow and is not included in a Brief or Digest purchase.
