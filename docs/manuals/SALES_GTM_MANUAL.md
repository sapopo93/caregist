# CareGist sales GTM manual

Updated 15 October 2026. For Henry, Sibongile and authorised sales staff. The immediate job is to establish whether a supplier has a real regional research or monitoring need and whether CareGist can fulfil it. This manual contains draft talk tracks, not permission to run an outreach campaign.

## Position the current offers

The buyer is a business selling into or advising the care sector. A care provider appearing in a shortlist is a research subject, not an assumed buyer of CareGist or a warm lead. Start with the buyer's decision, region and current research process.

| Buyer job | Offer | Published fee and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep track of supported public changes each week | Weekly Digest | £150 once, one England region, four weekly digests, no automatic renewal |
| Decide which accounts to investigate in a territory | Territory Opportunity Brief | £745 once, agreed scope, 25 to 50 distinct provider organisations, PDF/CSV/source manifest |
| Look up a provider and verify its official record | Free Directory | £0, public search |

No VAT is currently charged under the published wording; Henry confirms the actual order and invoice basis. Do not sell retired Radar subscriptions, paid listing promotion, bulk datasets or API entitlements from older packs. Do not promise contacts, CRM compatibility, exclusivity, procurement intent, compliance advice or revenue that the agreed product does not supply.

Draft introduction for an authorised conversation: “CareGist helps teams investigate care organisations using public CQC records. Are you trying to choose accounts in a territory, or keep up with changes in one region?”

CareGist is independent of CQC. Registration and rating facts do not predict inspections, demand, budget or future ratings. A prioritisation score is a research method, not a probability of purchase. Use current public claims and source dates; a saved sample is not current coverage proof.

Digest sales remain on hold until product-specific operative terms and fulfilment approval are evidenced. Use the published offer for suitability enquiries only while that hold remains. No terms or fulfilment approval is created by this revision.

## Qualify without repeating the buyer

Record the buyer's own answers, with source/time and unresolved questions:

1. What do you sell, and to which provider or service types?
2. Which territory or account decision are you making, and by when?
3. How do you research or monitor it today, and what is missing?
4. What facts would include or exclude an organisation? Which existing customers or groups should be excluded?
5. Which sample entry would you actually investigate, and why? What remains unknown?
6. Who approves a purchase and the scope? Does the relevant one-off fee fit this job?

Use asynchronous clarification where the enquiry is complete; a call is useful when there is genuine ambiguity. Choose **Brief**, **Digest**, **rescope**, **not a fit**, or **pending information**, with the next action, owner and date. The source owner checks feasibility. No unsupported category, inadequate provider count or unstaffed deadline should become an order.

## Demonstrate the evidence

Show a permitted sample through an approved route. Identify a provider, its scoped location(s), the source and dates, the factual reason for inclusion and the question still unanswered. If using the 11 October West Midlands demonstration, disclose its dated sources and illustrative buyer; it is not a live customer result, a typical market or proof of buying intent. Keep prospect data and unsent proposals private.

Draft answers for an authorised discussion:

| Buyer question | Supported response |
| --- | --- |
| “Are these ready-to-buy leads?” | “They are organisations to investigate. Public records do not tell us who intends to buy.” |
| “Can you guarantee 25 in my exact segment?” | “We check the distinct-provider set and exclusions before agreeing the quantity or asking for payment.” |
| “Will I receive it three days after this enquiry?” | “For a Brief, the target follows written scope confirmation and the source check. We agree an actual calendar date.” |
| “Can I upload the CSV to my CRM?” | “We supply a CSV. We need to check your field mapping and import before promising compatibility.” |
| “Can I order online?” | “Please send your scope. We confirm suitability, availability, terms and delivery before an approved payment request.” |
| “Does CQC endorse it?” | “No. CareGist is independent and links to the public source records.” |

## Agree a concrete next step

Capture the accepted written scope and its version. The [operations manual](OPERATIONS_MANUAL.md) defines G0 to G5 before any paid commitment or payment request, then G6 before release. The buyer's enthusiasm does not pass those gates. Do not issue an invoice, payment link or refund on your own authority.

For a Digest, agree one region, buyer type, four issue dates, supported signals, format, delivery route and terms. Include closures only where source evidence supports them; missing observations do not prove closure. For a Brief, freeze geography, provider/service predicates, time window, exclusions, counting/ranking, source, quantity, files, recipient, fee/terms and calendar date.

## Run only authorised outreach

Before a marketing call or send, verify campaign permission, recipient provenance, relevance, applicable channel controls, suppression and exact approved copy. CRM permission checks are necessary controls and do not independently authorise a campaign. Unknown or stale permission remains a hold. Stop on an opt-out and record it immediately in the approved system. Do not infer corporate status or permission from a public email address.

Use only an approved business identity, route and staffed reply queue. Recording and AI need their separate controls. Do not route around an amber/red call block or send manually to bypass a campaign restriction. Escalate complaints, money exceptions and unsupported requests to Henry. No contact, spend or external send is performed by preparing this manual.

## Measure outcomes truthfully

Use unique linked enquiry/order records in the private register. Keep researched, authorised contact, replied, qualified, scope accepted, payment requested, merchant-verified paid, reviewed release, usable receipt, acceptance and later usefulness separate. Record loss reasons and still-open cases.

CTA clicks are intent. Payment is a reconciled merchant record. Receipt is the agreed buyer confirming all required files open. Acceptance is the buyer confirming the agreed scope was met. Usefulness is the buyer's later stated decision/action. Do not convert any of these into another stage.

Review one matured cohort each week: enquiries, qualified decisions, accepted scopes, verified paid orders, actual labour, release/access timeliness, corrections and buyer-reported use. Show pending cases and the original/revised dates. There is no evidenced conversion uplift, optimum price or guaranteed capacity in this manual. Staff time spent training or recovering an order reduces prospecting/production capacity.
