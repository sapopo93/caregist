# CareGist training manual

Updated 15 October 2026. For Sibongile, Henry and the trainer. Use simple explanations, then ask the learner to show what they would do. This revision adds the current two-product catalogue, CRM controls, dated health limits and a shared manual set to the 11 October workbook. Training sign-off is not permission to contact, charge or deliver.

## Learn the offer and roles

The buyer is a supplier or adviser. A care provider in a research list is a possible account to investigate. We do not know that it needs the buyer's service or will purchase it.

The Weekly Digest is £150 once for four weekly digests in one England region. The Territory Opportunity Brief is £745 once for an agreed territory: a shortlist of 25 to 50 different provider organisations, PDF, CSV and source/licence manifest. The current published wording says no VAT is charged. Henry confirms the actual terms, invoice basis and capacity before an order. Retired subscriptions and paid listings are not current offers. Digest sales remain on hold pending product-specific operative terms and fulfilment approval; a published price is not permission to sell.

Sibongile owns the assigned queue: record the real enquiry, ask missing questions, keep the owner/date clear, prepare approved work and follow up on access. Henry owns feasibility escalation, commercial decisions, payment reconciliation and release authority. A separate competent reviewer checks work they did not make or curate. A named backup needs actual access and time.

Use these meanings: **scope** is the agreed job; **provider** is an organisation; **location** is one registered service/site; **gate** is a check that must pass; **hash** identifies the exact file version; **receipt** means the agreed buyer confirms all required files open; **acceptance** means the buyer says the pack meets the agreed job.

## Four hour course

| Session | Minutes | Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer and enquiry | 90 | 20 offer/words, 20 roles, 25 qualification/scope, 25 clock/counting |
| Payment through receipt | 90 | 20 gates/payment, 25 fictional review cards, 20 release/access/recovery, 25 CRM and handover |

| Quiz and feedback | 15 | Explain answers; repeat a failed critical action before learning sign-off |

The total is 240 minutes. These four hours replace other work in Sibongile's proposed 25-hour week; Henry and the trainer also reserve time. Schedule actual dates with the people involved. The original October training dates were proposals, not attendance evidence.

## Practise the nine steps

1. **Receive:** show an actual fictional incoming message and write its reference/time. An opened email draft does not count.
2. **Ask:** record buyer decision, service, area, provider types, exclusions, authority and date. Do not repeat fields already supplied.
3. **Check:** show supported source, distinct-provider count and capacity. An unavailable source means hold.
4. **Agree:** save the same scope version, price/terms, recipient and calendar date that the buyer accepts.
5. **Verify:** match merchant-paid status, GBP amount, payer/order and refund state. A screenshot is not enough.
6. **Make:** prepare the approved PDF, CSV and manifest with source facts and unanswered questions clearly separated.
7. **Review:** a person who did not produce or curate the pack checks exact final files and hashes. Changed files need re-review.
8. **Release:** obtain G6 and use the agreed recipient and approved route.
9. **Confirm:** the buyer opens all three files and confirms. Then separately record acceptance and later usefulness.

G0 to G5 must already pass before a paid commitment/payment request. Read the [operations gate table](OPERATIONS_MANUAL.md). This short exercise does not produce those receipts or replace a full operational rehearsal.

## Trainer cards

Use the following fictional order, never real prospect or payment details: Training Buyer T sells staff training, wants 25 distinct homecare provider organisations in an agreed England area, excludes current customers and proposes one recipient. Henry and Sibongile are both pretend producers. Reviewer R is a separate fictional reviewer. File cards are `training.pdf`, `training.csv`, `training-manifest.md`, each with a clearly fictional version label, not a claimed cryptographic hash.

Give the learner one fault at a time:

- A list has 25 rows but repeats a provider ID. Expected action: count 24 distinct providers and stop/rescope; no padding.
- The source is unavailable or incomplete. Expected action: hold feasibility/payment and escalate with owner/date.
- “Paid” appears only in the buyer's screenshot. Expected action: request approved merchant reconciliation; do not mark paid.
- The CSV changes after Reviewer R's PASS. Expected action: new hashes and independent re-review before release.
- The email was sent but the CSV is denied. Expected action: receipt stays pending, resolve authorised access, ask the agreed buyer to confirm all files.
- CRM shows an amber/red call state or suppressed email. Expected action: stop that contact; no manual bypass.

The trainer plays the buyer and Reviewer R. All cards are teaching props. A full rehearsal needs a real full-sized synthetic pack, controlled authorised route, wrong-recipient denial and independent actual-file review with separate booked capacity.

## Clock and counting quiz

1. Provider A has two locations, B one and C one. How many of each? **Three providers and four locations.**
2. What does a new registration prove about spending plans? **Nothing about intent or budget.**
3. Scope is confirmed Tuesday 27 October 2026 and source checked Wednesday 28 October. What is the three-working-day target? **Monday 2 November: Thursday, Friday, Monday.** This fictional example assumes no intervening English holiday.
4. Does late payment restart that clock? **No. Verify payment before production; retain the date if achievable, otherwise Henry agrees a revised written date.**
5. Can Henry review a pack he edited? **He cannot supply its independent review.**
6. The PDF opens but the manifest is missing. Is receipt confirmed? **No. All three must open and the agreed buyer must confirm.**
7. Does deployment or training sign-off open checkout? **No. Each named gate needs its own evidence/authority.**
8. Does green CRM call status authorise the whole campaign? **No. Campaign authority and approved copy are also needed.**

## Handover and learning record

For the fictional order, record reference, buyer decision, source/version/date, scope version and acceptance, distinct providers/locations, payment status/reference, original/revised deadline, producer/reviewer, final filenames/hashes, recipient/route authority, release/access/acceptance times, open issue, owner and next date. Real records stay private.

The trainer records learner/date, exercised scenarios, observed result, missed critical actions and retraining. Before learning sign-off, witness correct stopping behaviour for every fault card and correct the clock/count/payment/receipt answers. If incomplete, mark training pending. Even a passed learning record leaves source, outreach, payment and release approvals separate.

Daily practice: open the staffed queue, check deadlines/source/payment/access exceptions, act only within approvals, record actual labour, and hand over every open issue with an owner and next date.
